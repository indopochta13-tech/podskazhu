## 1. SUBJECT OF THE AGREEMENT
1.1. The Parties undertake to carry out gratuitous partnership cooperation aimed at the

fields of psychology, self-reflection, and soft-skills development.
1.2. Under this Agreement, the Partner shall, on a gratuitous basis, place a stationary
information button (hyperlink) inside its mobile application/service "[PARTNER_APP]"
leading to the informational page of the Developer's software "[DEVELOPER_APP]" at:
[DEVELOPER_APP_URL].
1.2.1. The software "[DEVELOPER_APP]" ([DEVELOPER_APP_DESC]) is published on the Developer's
informational website at [DEVELOPER_APP_URL] (download page: [DEVELOPER_APP_DOWNLOAD];
privacy policy: [DEVELOPER_APP_PRIVACY]). The hyperlink is reference/informational and
informs users about the Developer's product.
1.2.2. After publication of "[DEVELOPER_APP]" in the official RuStore marketplace, the Developer
notifies the Partner by email listed in Section 5. Within 14 calendar days of such notice,
the Partner updates the hyperlink to the official RuStore app page (or [DEVELOPER_APP_URL]

1.3. The Developer shall, on a gratuitous basis, provide full technical support for its
software "[DEVELOPER_APP]", ensure uninterrupted operation of AI models used (if applicable),
and advise users who come from the Partner's service.
1.4. This Agreement is gratuitous. The Parties do not pay each other remuneration. Each
Party bears its own expenses related to performance (including the Developer's costs for

## 2. LEGAL STATUS OF PRODUCTS AND PRICING
2.1. The software "[DEVELOPER_APP]" is the result of the Developer's personal intellectual
labor. All exclusive rights to code, structure, and algorithms belong to the Developer.
2.2. The Developer independently monetizes its software (including subscriptions and other
paid features through its own billing). All user payments go directly to the Developer's
account and acquiring. The Partner is not involved in payment processing and does not claim
agency commissions.
2.3. The Parties act as independent business subjects. The informational link integration
does not create a joint legal entity, simple partnership, or agency relationship requiring
licensing.

## 3. COMPLIANCE WITH FAS AND ROSKOMNADZOR REQUIREMENTS
3.1. Placement of the link to the Developer's software in the Partner's interface is purely
reference/informational, does not unduly promote the Developer's product, and per FAS RF

3.2. The Parties independently bear responsibility before Roskomnadzor for personal data
law compliance (Federal Law No. 152-FZ). The Partner is not responsible for user data
processing inside the Developer's software when such software operates autonomously and
the Developer acts as the operator.
